# Application description

Project name: **Embodied Safety Toolkit**

Role: **Graduate Researcher — Collaborative Research / Portfolio Engineering**

> Developed a reproducible scenario-to-report workflow for embodied-agent safety, building on our team's SENTINEL evaluation modules. The toolkit generates controlled safety cases, records action/state traces, checks grounded safety rules, and reports violations with step-level evidence. My work includes safety-scenario generation and setup fixes in the research project, plus the standalone pipeline, validation, testing, and documentation in this portfolio edition.

When discussing the project, explain the overall research problem, the team architecture, your own scenario/debugging work and the added engineering in this repository. A reusable template can generate many cases; template count is not the total scope of the research.

The 24-case suite is synthetic regression coverage, not a model safety benchmark. Cite the team's original paper for research context and describe the repository's measured results separately.
