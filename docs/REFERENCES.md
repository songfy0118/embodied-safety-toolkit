# Research references

## SENTINEL

Simon Sinong Zhan, Philip Wang, Yao Liu and collaborators. *SENTINEL: A Multi-Level Formal Framework for Safety Evaluation of Foundation Model-based Embodied Agents.* arXiv:2510.12985. The arXiv page lists v3 dated July 20, 2026.

- [Paper and version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12985)
- [Official project page](https://nu-ideas-lab.github.io/SENTINEL/)
- [Public trajectory-level source](https://github.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/SENTINEL/tree/ai2thor)
- [Source commit used for comparison](https://github.com/NU-IDEAS-Lab/SENTINEL/commit/0cfa2739f0783f0f058a62d2334d8b49a5d66cf1)

The paper motivates the semantic/plan/trajectory organization. This repository focuses on the scenario and trajectory workflow and does not claim to reproduce the paper's tables or all three levels. The project-page author list and arXiv version may differ; use the paper's current citation export when citing the full author list.

This derivative repository and its new fixture results should not be cited as official SENTINEL benchmark results. Feiyang's participation in subsequent team research is separate from authorship of the linked published version.
